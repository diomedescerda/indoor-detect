Interpretation limits:
- A wider view can contain more targets. GT per image and TP per image accompany recall so a narrow view is not rewarded for hiding difficult targets.
- No cross-image object identity or task relevance was annotated; the study cannot count real-world targets outside the frame.
- The 33 groups are paired acquisitions, not necessarily independent rooms/homes. Paired bootstrap intervals resample groups and may be optimistic when groups share environments.
- Pairwise intervals are exploratory, unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Choosing a winner on this test set makes it a configuration-selection set; validate the choice on new scenes.
- iPhone 0.5x/1x results may not transfer to the Android phone’s optics, camera resolution or framing.
- Desktop proxy uses a different runtime and image decoder/resizer from Android. Android replay and phone timing are required before a final app recommendation.
- Confirm the six-photo sequence labels against capture notes before relying on the comparison.
