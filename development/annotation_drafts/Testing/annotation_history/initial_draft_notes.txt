AssistiveVision model-assisted annotation draft

All 198 photos were inspected directly in the Codex conversation. No YOLO,
EfficientDet, or OpenAI API was used. GPT-6.1 Sol was the user's requested
chat model; the scripts do not independently establish the chat model identity.

preannotations.coco.json is a DRAFT, not reviewed ground truth.
Boxes were visually estimated on 384x512 previews and scaled by 4 to the
1536x2048 originals. Small/occluded objects and box edges need careful correction
at full resolution. The terrace and office scenes are particularly dense.

Categories use true COCO IDs:
1 person; 62 chair; 63 couch; 65 bed; 67 dining table; 70 toilet;
72 tv; 73 laptop; 81 sink; 82 refrigerator.
Desktop monitors are treated as tv (COCO tv/monitor convention).
Office desks, benches, side tables, cabinets, bathtubs, and paintings are outside
these classes. Reflections were not intentionally duplicated as extra instances.
Truncated objects are included when recognizable. Overlapping bed boundaries
and partially hidden chairs should receive extra review.

Image IDs follow ascending IMG filename numbers, not contiguous filename numbers.
Six-capture cycle: head harness 1x, head harness 0.5x, chest harness 1x,
chest harness 0.5x, gorillapod 1x, gorillapod 0.5x.
There are 33 provisional groups. Confirm the ordering against your capture notes.

Review files:
- review/annotated_group_01.jpg through annotated_group_33.jpg: six-photo overlays
- review/annotated_IMG_*.jpeg: individual preview overlays
- review/group_01.jpg through group_33.jpg: unannotated contact sheets
- review_checklist.csv: one row per photo, all initially unreviewed
- capture_groups.csv: capture metadata and source hashes

Roboflow import package: review_bundle.zip contains data/*.jpeg and
preannotations.coco.json. Use COCO annotations when importing; verify association
on a few images before completing the upload. No cloud upload has been performed.
The local review overlays provide an alternative for examining the draft, but
editing bounding boxes requires an annotation editor such as Roboflow or CVAT.

Validation:
  .venv/bin/python verify_gt.py preannotations.coco.json

After you review and correct EVERY photo, including empty images, export COCO
at original resolution and convert by category name:
  .venv/bin/python build_ground_truth.py /path/to/reviewed-export.json --reviewed-all
  .venv/bin/python verify_gt.py labels.json

Do not pass --reviewed-all before completing review. labels.json is deliberately
not created from the unreviewed draft. Model benchmarking requires reviewed GT
and the model/evaluation files, which are absent from this workspace.

The .gitignore excludes photos, annotation JSON, review artifacts, the venv,
and common .env secret files. No commits were made.
